# Surface brief: app shell (layout/base.html + pager)

Mode: Operate. Applies to every page of LeafCare. Audience: home gardeners, one hand on a phone. Task: reach "Quét lá" fast, browse plants/diseases/scans, page through lists.

## Direction contract

THESIS: LeafCare is a small plant clinic: clean enamel-white surfaces, a committed clinic-green shell, every control labeled. Refuses the generic white SaaS navbar and also the pharmacy-logo look: the cross becomes a leaf mark of its own.

OWN-WORLD: Clinic green (#0B7A4B) shell on enamel white (#F4F8F5), deep green-black ink (#14261C), red only for errors/delete. One workhorse face (Be Vietnam Pro, built for Vietnamese). Rounded-square plate for the logo with a white cross whose top arm is a leaf. Hairline green-tinted borders, flat surfaces, soft low shadows only on floating bars.

STORY: The visitor sees at once where they are (active tab), what the one main action is ("Quét lá"), and how to move between Cây, Bệnh, Lịch sử. They trust it as a careful tool and act with one thumb.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: solid green top bar, logo plate left, nav links (Trang chủ, Cây, Bệnh, Lịch sử), white "Quét lá" button right; content on enamel white; slim footer. Phone (390): slim green top bar with logo; fixed bottom bar with four tabs and a large raised "Quét lá" button in the center, inside the thumb zone.

FORM: assigned, candidate 3 of the grounded list (clinic signage), seed:dac01369c1762868.

## User steer (chosen on the decision page)

Turn the cross into LeafCare's own leaf mark, not a pharmacy logo. The primary "Quét lá" button must be easy to press one-handed on a phone.
